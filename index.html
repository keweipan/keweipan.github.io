Research

My research lies at the intersection of non-commutative probability theory and random matrix theory. I study large deviation principles for random matrix ensembles and their connections to entropy and functional inequalities in non-commutative probability. A particular focus of my work is Boolean independence, including the development of entropy and Fisher information in the Boolean setting, the study of associated functional inequalities, and the analysis of limit theorems such as the Boolean central limit theorem.
